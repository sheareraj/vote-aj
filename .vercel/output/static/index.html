Priorities for Monaghan Ward

Pay attention to the things that affect daily life.

Residents should be able to see what their councillor is working on, why it matters, and what progress is being made. These are the areas where I intend to put most of my attention.

Affordability & Responsible Government

Keep pressure on household tax bills by watching how quickly City costs grow, planning infrastructure before it becomes an emergency, and asking whether taxpayers are getting good value for what they spend.

Read More →

Economic Growth & Employment Lands

Grow the local job and business base so more of the City's costs are shared beyond homeowners. That means making investment easier and working to make more land ready for employers and job-creating businesses.

Read More →

Safer Streets & Better Outcomes

Focus on whether our public-safety and homelessness responses are actually making streets, neighbourhoods, businesses, and public spaces safer - not just on how much activity or spending is taking place.

Read More →
A City People Want to Live In

Recreation, Arts & Quality of Life

Good representation is also about protecting the everyday things that make a neighbourhood worth living in - parks, recreation, sports, arts, culture, and the community groups that bring people together.

Read More →
How I'll Make Decisions

Before I vote on something, I want four questions answered.

Residents should not need to decode City Hall to understand a decision. I want the problem, the expected result, the side effects, and the cost explained in plain language.

1. What problem are we actually trying to solve?
2. Will this make things better - and how will we know?
3. What behaviour will this decision encourage?
4. What will it cost, and what are we giving up?

About AJ

Local roots. Practical experience. Accessible representation.

My family has roots in the Peterborough area spanning generations. I'm a husband, father, business professional, and long-time member of the community, and my wife Seanna and I are raising our two boys here. I know representation starts with being available, listening, and staying connected to what is happening in the ward.

My background in engineering, business intelligence, data analytics, change management, and real estate gives me tools to work through complex issues. The point of those skills is simple: help residents get clearer answers, better follow-through, and decisions they can understand.
