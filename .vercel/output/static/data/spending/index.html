Where is the growth showing up?

Department names change. Services are more useful. These comparisons follow municipal services that can be matched cleanly from 2022 to 2026 and show the change in their budgeted net requirement.

Largest comparable increases

The biggest dollar changes in stable five-year series.

The trend measure is the City’s Net Requirement Before Indirect Revenues: the amount left after direct service revenues, before allocated indirect revenue is applied. It is neither gross spending nor the final Net Tax Levy.

How to read the measure

For example, the City’s 2026 table shows Police Services at $41.3M in Net Requirement Before Indirect Revenues, then −$2.7M in Allocated Indirect Revenue, producing a $38.5M Net Tax Levy. The five-year comparisons below deliberately use the first measure consistently.

Important limitation

Some City reorganizations cannot be compared cleanly across all five years.

Public Works, recreation/culture, planning and corporate administration have changed structure. The detailed explorer uses shorter comparison windows rather than pretending those reorganizations are real spending jumps.
