Why 2025 is flagged

The accounting presentation changes materially.

The Yard line moves from essentially a fully recovered activity in 2024 to a $2.59M net requirement in the 2025 approved figures. Winter Control falls while Surface Services rises sharply at the same time. That pattern is consistent with changed allocation/recovery treatment, so the full +$3.32M is not labelled as organic service growth.

One known 2025 operating change

Municipal-lot snow plowing moved in-house.

The City's service-delivery report identifies $473K of existing municipal-lot budget transferred into Public Works, plus $432K of additional 2025 staff/material/operating cost. Including the initial 2024 amount, the report describes an annualized incremental operating increase of about $500K.

The transferred budget is a classification move, not new City-wide spending. This known change explains only part of the 2025 Public Works structural break.
