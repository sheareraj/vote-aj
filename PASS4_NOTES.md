# Peterborough By The Numbers — Pass 4 notes

## Important correction: Legacy Fund levy support

Earlier project copy described the extra 2026 $3M Legacy Fund Income Retention draw as though it were the first time accumulated Legacy income had been used to reduce the tax increase. That was incorrect and has been fixed in this pass.

The official record shows:

- 2024 budget: $800,000 Legacy Fund Income Reserve draw approved during Finance Committee to reduce the proposed all-inclusive tax-rate increase by 0.40 percentage points.
- Final 2024 budget: Council added a further one-time $510,000 Legacy Fund Income Reserve draw, bringing Legacy-income tax-rate support to $1.31M.
- 2025 budget: the $1.31M prior-year Legacy reserve support was not carried forward.
- 2026 budget: Council used an additional $3M from accumulated Legacy Fund Income Retention reserve, reducing the 2026 tax requirement by 1.32 percentage points.
- Preliminary 2027 budget work identifies a $3M base-budget correction associated with the one-time 2026 draw.

The dashboard now distinguishes the regular Legacy investment-income contribution to capital from these one-time levy offsets.

## Public Works normalization

The reported Public Works net requirement is:

- 2023: $9,339,586
- 2024: $9,718,320
- 2025 Approved: $13,040,487
- 2026: $13,661,707

The 2024→2025 jump is NOT treated as a clean measure of service-cost growth. Component presentation changes materially: the Yard activity moves from essentially fully recovered to a $2.591M net requirement, while Winter Control and Surface Services allocations also shift.

One specifically documented change is the move to in-house municipal-lot snow plowing:

- $473,309 existing municipal-lot budget transferred into Public Works for 2025 (reclassification, not new city-wide spending).
- $431,501 additional 2025 staff/material/operating cost.
- $68,000 incremental operating cost initiated in 2024.
- Annualized incremental operating increase identified by the report: about $499,501.

The remainder of the 2024→2025 Public Works structural break remains deliberately unclassified pending a fully sourced allocation bridge. Component rows differ from the published total by -$1 in 2023 and +$1 in 2024; the dashboard preserves the official total and treats this as immaterial source-line rounding/extraction variance.
